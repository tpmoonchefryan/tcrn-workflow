# TCRN Workflow 1.1.3

This append-only patch release preserves the v1.1.2 event, storage, and CLI
protocols. A time-attestation store smaller than one segment keeps the single
`000001` segment, byte-identical to what v1.1.2 wrote.

## Repairs

- Time-attestation store (TCRN-CROSS-INC-378). The store digest is streamed
  instead of being computed over one canonical document, so a store is no
  longer bounded by the 1 MiB input limit that stopped the cross-project
  receipts after event 8249. Segments roll at 1 MiB or 8,192 records, every
  byte is computed before anything is written, unreferenced segments are
  removed, and each read-modify-write holds a directory lock with stale-holder
  takeover.
- Writes that carry `--attest-dir` check the clock, the directory, and the
  attestation store before the lease. A receipt failure after the event is
  committed is reported as committed, with the version and head event hash.
- The read-only `attestation-verify` verb reports whether a store is
  consistent with its chain. `attestation-migrate --mode repair` and
  `--mode restore` repair a store against its chain, or restore a backup,
  without hand edits. `specs/time-attestation-v1.md` specifies the segmented
  store.
- Proof of the repair: the story-340 two-process writers pipe their stderr and
  show it in both failure messages (TCRN-CROSS-INC-379), and the INC-155
  coverage-integrity verbatim block is re-recorded after the new repair test
  joined the coverage baseline (TCRN-CROSS-INC-380).
- Managed hooks propagate the governed platform root. The SessionStart and
  Stop wrappers pass `--container-root`, and on Codex the dispatch-telemetry,
  agents-zero, and ssh-write-observer handlers accept it instead of deriving
  the root from their install location (TCRN-CROSS-INC-353,
  TCRN-CROSS-INC-368).
- Bounded search resolves real paths before judging scope and closes the
  platform-root and home ancestor bypass (TCRN-CROSS-INC-359,
  TCRN-CROSS-INC-362).
- Query-language resolution no longer treats a query already in the artifact
  language as outside it (TCRN-CROSS-INC-369).

## Proof budget

`coreSourceLineCap` rises from 23815 to the measured 24126 lines after the
attestation repair (Owner ruling OQ-S6D-2 A, recorded in the policy
exceptions, with no margin added). The proof-to-product ratio is
71002/28460 = 2.4948 against the measured hard ratio 2.5324.

## Known limitations

- A stale attestation lock is judged only by whether its recorded pid is
  alive. If a writer is killed while holding the lock and another long-lived
  process reuses its pid before takeover, the lock keeps looking held: writes
  with `--attest-dir` in that partition are refused before the lease until
  that process exits, and no verb clears such a lock (INIT-051 issue list
  #214).
- An empty lock file, or one whose pid was never written, is also treated as
  held, with the same effect (issue list #216). Both cases fail closed and
  lose no data.
- `validateEventChain` still rejects a chain longer than 10,000 events.
  Ordinary reads replay from snapshots and are unaffected, but a replay
  without a usable snapshot (a fresh checkout, a missing or invalid snapshot,
  or a replay from genesis) fails on a longer chain, and platform-doctor's
  chain headroom does not warn before that point (issue list #217).

The immutable v1.1.2 Engine and v1.0.4 Helper publications remain unchanged.
This release is published only after exact-commit CI succeeds and its tags,
assets, bootstrap, and downloaded digests are independently verified.
